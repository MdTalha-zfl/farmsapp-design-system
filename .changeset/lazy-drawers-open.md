Add Drawer (Drawer, DrawerHeader, DrawerBody, DrawerFooter) — a right-edge panel built on floating-ui, with `isOpen`/`onDismiss` control, an optional backdrop with scroll lock and click-outside dismissal (`showOverlay`), `Escape` to dismiss (gated by `isDismissible`), a floating close button when there's no `DrawerHeader`, and `isLazy` to choose between unmounting on close (default) or staying mounted and hidden so children keep their state.
