Add BottomNav (BottomNav, BottomNavItem) — a full-width, edge-to-edge bottom navigation bar for 2 to 5 primary destinations, with the active item's icon shown in a filled circle and its label in bold. `BottomNavItem` renders a link when given `href` (accepting a polymorphic `as` for a router's link component) or a button otherwise, and `BottomNav` warns in development if given fewer than 2 or more than 5 items.
